How to play online with Fiveserver:
===================================


1. If you use Kitserver 5.4.0, then all you need to do
is modify your network.cfg, and set these options correctly:

network.server = <your-fiveserver-host>
network.stun-server = "www.stunserver.org"


2. If you don't use Kitserver, then you will need to hexedit
your WE9LEK.exe file so that it tries to connect to your server.

Step 1: (You only need to do it once)
In your hosts file, add this line at the end:

132.177.123.13    stun.we9le.konami-asia.com

Step 2: If you don't already have an account on Fiveserver,
fill out the registration form and click "Register":
http://<your-fiveserver-host>:8180/form

Step 3. Modify WE9LEK.exe that you use to launch the game:

At offset 0x6d8f20, you should see this: 61.82.71.50\0
Change it to be: <your-fiveserver-host>\0 
(make sure you have the 0-byte, after your hostname)

Step 4. Go to 'Network', then when asked for serial number
and password, make sure you enter the same serial number that
you have registered with. For password, it is important to 
enter both your username and password, separated by dash ('-')

For example: 
Username: durak66
Password: glukalovo

You must enter: durak66-glukalovo
